Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the data come from?

We aggregate data from public APIs including NASA, SpaceX, TheSpaceDevs (Launch Library 2), and various space news RSS feeds.

Is this a commercial website?

No! Space Program is a non-commercial passion project. We don't run ads, sell data, or generate any revenue. It's made by enthusiasts, for enthusiasts.

Can I contribute?

We welcome feedback, suggestions, and bug reports! Feel free to reach out via email if you'd like to contribute ideas or improvements.

How often is data updated?

Launch data is fetched in real-time from APIs when you visit the site. News feeds are refreshed regularly to bring you the latest space updates.
